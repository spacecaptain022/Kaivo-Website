**TIMELINE: V1 OF DESIGN BY EOP FRIDAY 10 APRIL**

**KAIVO one-page website**

**PAGE ORDER**

Hero → trust strip → problem → delegation shift → demo showdown → Kaivo speaks → approval/trust → value cards → waitlist campaign → token→ why now → bigger company thesis → about → team → final CTA

**DESIGN NOTES**

* Keep the page emotionally premium, not crypto-y, not corporate-y  
* Very large typography  
* Air, calm, confidence  
* Warm neutrals with black, soft grey, and selective travel accent colours  
* Show real product moments early  
* Keep “Ask. Approve. Done.” as a supporting mnemonic lower on the page, not the hero  
* Top half \= consumer desire  
* Bottom half \= category creation \+ investor logic
